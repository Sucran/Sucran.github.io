Welcome to the Tech Sharing section!

I've been working in technology since 2019, during which I've participated in various types of projects, such as: Distributed Job Scheduling Systems, Cloud-Native Streaming Lakehouse Systems, Big Data Platforms (with a focus on job orchestration and diagnostics), and RAG Systems.

Recently, I've been particularly interested in the AI field. I often absorb the latest system information through podcasts, but retrieving and summarizing podcasts isn't easy to handle. Therefore, I decided to create two open-source projects:
- [ModalTranscriberMcp](https://github.com/Sucran/modal-transcriber-mcp): A serverless remote MCP service for audio transcription, capable of using multiple GPUs for fast audio transcription.
- [PodcastAgent](https://github.com/Sucran/podcast-agent): A podcast agent based on the OpenAI-Agents-SDK, a vertical agent that integrates multiple functions such as downloading, transcribing, translating, and text-to-speech.
